    On keeping paper

    The best sheets are the ones that waited. A little foxing, a warp from a damp summer, ink that has lost its blue. I do not want paper that pretends it was made this morning.

    A preference

    Quiet type, a wide margin, nothing that winks. If it would look foolish in a drawer in fifty years, it does not belong on the desk now.

    Later

    A list of books. A sentence that would not sit still. This pad will take them without comment.
