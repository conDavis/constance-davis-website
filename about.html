    At home for letters

    Const

    small work · quiet rooms

    I keep old paper because it already knows how to hold a life: a crease, a stain, a name pressed too hard. I make things in that spirit — writing, pictures, the occasional site that would rather be a letter.

    The facts can wait on the reverse: where I live, what I do. The card is enough for now.
